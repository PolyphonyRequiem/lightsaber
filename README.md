## The Philosophy

There is an old truth among the Jedi: **you build your own lightsaber.**

Not because there are no factories. Not because the Order couldn't stamp them out on an assembly line, identical and interchangeable. They could. But they *don't* — because the act of construction *is* the act of understanding. The kyber crystal doesn't just power the blade; it resonates with the one who found it, who meditated upon it, who learned to listen to its frequency before ever asking it to sing.

**We are not Jedi. We are something stranger.**

We are software engineers standing at the event horizon of artificial intelligence — a threshold where the tools we wield have begun to wield themselves. Code generates code. Models dream in functions. The machine whispers suggestions before our fingers reach the keys, and sometimes — *sometimes* — it whispers correctly.

In this neon-lit dawn, it would be easy to let the machine build everything. To accept the factory-pressed hilt, the mass-produced blade. To become an operator instead of a craftsman. A *prompter* instead of an *engineer*.

This repository is a rejection of that comfort.

## What This Is

**Lightsaber** is a personal engineering toolkit — bespoke, opinionated, and alive. It is the sum of every configuration, every script, every workflow that transforms a general-purpose terminal into *my* terminal. A general-purpose editor into *my* editor. A general-purpose mind into *my* mind augmented.

It is, in the tradition of the Jedi, a weapon I built with my own hands — so that when I ignite it, I understand every photon in the blade.

But we aren't monks in robes. We're cyberpunks in the glow of multiple monitors at 2 AM, jacked into systems that span the planet. So this lightsaber hums with a frequency the Old Republic never imagined:

- **🔧 Configurations** — The crystal at the core. Editor settings, shell configs, keybindings — the resonance frequency of how I think translated into how the machine responds.
- **⚡ Scripts & Automations** — The emitter matrix. Workflows that turn intention into action with minimal friction. The difference between swinging a blade and *cutting*.
- **🧠 AI Integrations** — The cortical jack. Patterns for working *with* intelligence — not surrendering to it, but augmenting the hand that holds the hilt.
- **🛡️ Standards & Patterns** — The discipline. Linting rules, commit conventions, architectural decisions. The forms and katas that keep the blade from cutting its wielder.
- **🔬 Experiments** — The workshop. Half-forged ideas, prototypes, things that glow but don't yet cut. The workbench where the next iteration takes shape.

## The Cyberpunk Addendum

Here is the uncomfortable truth the Jedi Council never spoke aloud:

**The lightsaber is not a symbol of peace. It is a symbol of autonomy.**

A Jedi who builds their own lightsaber is a Jedi who cannot be disarmed by having their supply chain disrupted. A software engineer who builds their own tools is an engineer who cannot be made obsolete by the next platform shift, the next API deprecation, the next corporate pivot that orphans a million workflows overnight.

In the age of AI, this is not nostalgia. It is *survival architecture*.

The corporations will sell you lightsabers. They will be beautiful. They will be convenient. They will lock you into a crystal you don't understand, a hilt that phones home, a blade that dims when your subscription lapses.

**Build your own.**

Not because it's faster. Not because it's easier. Because when the grid goes dark and the APIs return 403 and the models hallucinate and the platform you built your career on announces end-of-life — you will still have a blade that ignites.

And you will understand every photon in it.
