# Lore

A knowledge base of canonical Star Wars material on lightsabers, the Jedi, and the construction of both. Captured to ground this project in something deeper than aesthetic. Used as a reference when reasoning about the toolkit, when consulting the council, and when writing about any of it.

Both Disney canon and Legends are valid sources. Where they differ, the documents say so explicitly.

## Reading Order

Start with `philosophy.md` if you want the spiritual foundation. Start with `anatomy.md` if you want the mechanics. Start with `gathering-and-construction.md` if you want the ritual. They all loop back to the same thing.

## Documents

### `philosophy.md`
The Jedi Code (both versions), the Sith Code, Grey/Bendu/Je'daii balance traditions, Living Force vs Cosmic Force, attachment vs love, the role of compassion, what mastery actually means. The spiritual heart of the lore base.

### `anatomy.md`
The components of a lightsaber: emitter shroud, focusing lens, crystal chamber, power cell, hilt, pommel, modulator, conduit. How they integrate. Why hilts vary by wielder. Failure modes. Why no two sabers are identical.

### `kyber.md`
Kyber crystals: origin, Force-attunement, locations (Ilum, Jedha, Lothal, Christophsis, Mustafar). Bonding with the wielder. Why crystals are colorless until claimed. Color meanings. The bleeding process. Purification (Ahsoka's white blades).

### `gathering-and-construction.md`
The Gathering ceremony at the Crystal Caves of Ilum. Huyang and the Crucible. The Padawan's trial and crystal selection. The construction itself. The training arc from Initiate to Master. Variations: Ezra's self-built saber, Cal Kestis, Sith bleeding, Knights of Ren.

### `forms-and-variants.md`
The seven forms of lightsaber combat (Shii-Cho through Juyo/Vaapad). Hilt and blade variants (saberstaff, crossguard, lightwhip, dual-phase, shoto, Darksaber, training sabers). Color symbolism in combat context.

### `mentorship-and-lineage.md`
The Master-Padawan bond and how it's formed. Training methodology and the Trials. Notable lineages (Yoda → Dooku → Qui-Gon → Obi-Wan → Anakin → Ahsoka → Sabine). The pass-it-down nature of knowledge. Mentorship across the grey.

### `notable-sabers-and-duels.md`
Individual sabers as objects with their own stories: the Skywalker saber, the Darksaber, Obi-Wan's blades through his life, Ahsoka's pair, Vader's saber, Luke's green saber, Kylo's crossguard, Ezra's self-built saber, Cal Kestis's dual-phase. Pivotal duels and what they taught.

## Why This Exists

Two reasons.

First, the lightsaber project (this repo) is grounded in a metaphor. Metaphors leak unless they're well understood. This is the homework on the metaphor.

Second, the council (the persona agents in `~/.copilot/agents/council-*.agent.md`) draw on this material when asked. Yoda quotes Yoda. Ahsoka knows what she did with the Sixth Brother's crystals. Obi-Wan remembers Mustafar. The lore makes the council voices real instead of generic.

See `REFLECTIONS.md` for the explicit mapping of lore concepts to toolkit construction practice.
